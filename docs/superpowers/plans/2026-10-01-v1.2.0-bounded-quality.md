### Formal result recognition

- A PDF result heading requires punctuation/parenthesis or an explicit line break
  after its kind/number. Discussion such as `Theorem 1.4 also supplies ...` is not
  another declaration. This is a bounded layout heuristic, not semantic parsing.
- Genuine repeated formal numbers retain separate IDs and ambiguity. They are not
  deduplicated to make correspondence navigation succeed.
- Isolated headings may join one substantive block within four adjacent spans,
  skipping page counters/uppercase running headers and stopping at proofs/results
  or section headings. Heading/body locations are both retained, including across
  a page boundary. Multi-block mathematical statement/proof reconstruction remains
  a limitation; no claim of complete PDF extraction is made.
- Functional pre-change RED: 2 failed / 2 passed. Focused formal/PDF/evidence/
  correspondence group: 23 passed in 4.70s.
